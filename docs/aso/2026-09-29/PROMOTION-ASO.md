## One proposed listing experiment

Name: `EN Daily verse benefit 2026-10`

- Type: localized English listing experiment, one variant only.
- Control: current short description, **Daily Bible verses, verse images, Bible quiz and a quiz certificate generator.**
- Candidate: **Find hope in a daily Bible verse. Share verse images, quiz and PDF certificates.**
- Hypothesis: connecting the primary daily-verse intent to a clear personal benefit will improve install intent compared with a feature-only sentence.
- Change only the short description. Keep title, full description, icon, screenshot set/order and video stable during the experiment.
- Measure the exact target metric available in Console, preferably unique user install clicks for listing intent, alongside completed acquisitions and subsequent use as separate outcomes. An install click is not a completed install.
- Start only after the initial observation period, after any necessary screenshot accuracy correction has settled, and when Console's experiment-duration calculator suggests an actionable test. With 38 aggregate listing visitors in the latest measured 28-day window, there is currently insufficient evidence for a useful quick result. Do not invent a statistical minimum or declare a winner after a few installs.
- Run at least a complete weekly cycle and until Console provides a sufficiently conclusive result. If traffic remains sparse, retain the control rather than choosing by a small apparent uplift. Record dates, sample sizes, confidence information and any simultaneous campaign/release changes.
- English localization is not an India-only experiment. Scope results to the actual audience served; do not claim an India/Malayalam lift from its aggregate result.

Google's experiments support localized text and graphic variants and an estimated duration. [Experiment setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12053285?hl=en). Accurate, useful descriptions avoid repetitive keywords and unsupported claims. [Metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en).

## Measurement correction and ongoing ASO

Google's current definitions put generic category searches into **Explore**. In store-listing reports, **Search** covers app-name/closely associated brand searches. Grow overview's **Paid and direct** combines brand searches, ads and referrals. Consequently, the six Explore acquisitions in today's report cannot be labeled browsing-only, and the seven Paid/direct acquisitions cannot be attributed to advertising alone. Search-term-level evidence is needed to isolate “daily bible verse.” This interpretation supplements today's observation without changing its measured numbers.

Tagged links support UTM source/campaign reporting where available. View them under ads/referrals in the listing report, and record clicks separately from completed acquisitions in Statistics. Small sources may be grouped under Other; missing rows are not zero. [Current reporting definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859173?hl=en).

Continue the existing daily automation; do not create another. Preserve the signed-in Chrome/India language context and exact baseline queries. Evaluate seven consecutive daily top-five primary samples as the ranking milestone, plus completed equal 7-day and 28-day report windows as data matures. Keep campaign traffic, brand discovery and generic discovery distinct. The next decision is based on evidence, not a daily title rewrite.
