## Features and visual evidence

The shipped app supports daily Bible verse cards, Daily Hope, verse images, favorites, verse-linked journal notes, reminders and Bible Quiz. Version 36 documents randomized quiz rounds, Scripture references, answer review, language matching, result sharing, PDF export and printing. The app is free with ads and offers optional one-time ad removal. Do not imply a complete chapter-by-chapter Bible reader, a devotional library, a prayer community or a subscription. Promotional copy should not advertise a question count.

The later local debug changes include removed gallery category counts, the compact quiz header and pinned answer action, the quiz-start language correction, direct sharing of a full PDF attachment and four additional image backgrounds. PDF export was already released; changing the Share action from score text to an attached PDF is a later local change. Do not describe these refinements as released without a subsequent verified production release.

Original approved assets exist for seven locales under `assets/store/`: six phone images, two tablet images, one feature graphic and a localized video per locale. The final real-device quiz screenshots are `output/quiz-launch-2026-09-27/store-upload/A16-<locale>-01-quiz-question.png` and `A16-<locale>-03-quiz-results.png`. The A16 captures supersede the older emulator files with similar names. Prior release notes recorded their submission; the initial check at the start of this ASO task found no remaining unpublished changes from that earlier submission.

The initial check found a live visual-coverage gap: **English had 8/8 phone screenshots, while Malayalam had only 2/8**, showing the quiz question and quiz result. The Malayalam Console showed the warning to provide at least four screenshots for promotion. This motivated restoring accurate core-feature coverage alongside the two quiz images; meeting the image count alone is not proof of promotion eligibility or a promise of promotion.

Direct file inspection found all six Malayalam core PNGs under `assets/store/screenshots/phone/ml-IN/` are **1080 × 2424**, not the 1080 × 1920 claimed by the README. Prepared replacement sets under `output/aso-2026-09-27/en-US/` and `ml-IN/` contain eight **1080 × 1920**, opaque RGB PNGs each. A new aspect-fit renderer uniformly scales each complete approved source onto an ivory canvas without cropping content, stretching, reconstructing the UI or rewriting text. The order is Scripture, Daily Hope, quiz question, quiz result, draw a card, favorites, journal and themes. Both contact sheets and full-resolution English/Malayalam Scripture and quiz images were visually checked, and geometry, opacity, source aspect ratio and counts were verified programmatically. Original files are preserved. `output/aso-2026-09-27/screenshot-manifest.json` records mappings and hashes. Upload and publication remain the root task's separate responsibility; preparation alone does not establish live replacement or Google promotion.

Preserve approved visuals unless a specific tested change is justified. The existing asset validator assumes the original six-image phone sets and does not validate the supplemental quiz screenshots.
