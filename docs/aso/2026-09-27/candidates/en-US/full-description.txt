Hope Cards brings you a daily Bible verse, a Bible quiz and beautiful verse images in one calm app. Read today's verse in Daily Hope, save Scripture that matters to you and share encouragement with family and friends.

Verse of the day and Daily Hope
Make time for Scripture in your daily routine. Open Daily Hope for today's Bible verse, draw a verse card when you want a quiet moment and set a daily reminder for a time that suits you.

Bible quiz with answer review
Explore Bible quiz questions, check your answers and learn from the Scripture references. Review a completed round to revisit what you learned.

Bible verse images to share
Browse the verse image gallery, find a design you like and share a Bible verse image with family or friends. Keep encouraging words close through clear typography and peaceful artwork.

Favorites and personal journal
Save favorite verses and add your own reflection, prayer or journal note beside each verse. Return to your saved Scripture and thoughts whenever you need them.

English, Malayalam and more
The app supports English, Malayalam, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Filipino. Read Malayalam Bible verses from Sathyavedapusthakam (1910), or choose another supported translation. English editions include Berean Standard Bible, Bible in Basic English, King James Version and World English Bible.

Made for a simple daily habit
Enjoy card themes, optional background music in Daily Hope and comfortable reading on phones and tablets. Back up and restore your favorites, journal entries and settings. No account is needed.

Ads and purchases
The app's features are available to everyone. Ads support Hope Cards. An optional one-time Google Play purchase removes ads permanently. There is no subscription.

Take a quiet moment with Hope Cards: read, reflect, learn and share.
