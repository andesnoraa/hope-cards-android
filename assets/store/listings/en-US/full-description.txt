Start each day with a Bible verse of the day for hope, reflection and encouragement, one beautiful Scripture card at a time.

Hope Cards is a calm daily Bible verse app for a simple Scripture habit. Draw a Bible verse card, read today’s verse in Daily Hope, set a daily Bible reminder, save favorite Scripture and add journal notes linked to each verse. There is no complicated reading plan and no account to create.

Bible quiz in your language
Explore Bible people, places and events in English, Malayalam, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Filipino. Enjoy randomized rounds that follow your selected Bible translation. Check each answer with a Scripture reference and review your score. Share a PDF report with every question, your selected answer and the correct answer. Quiz questions work offline.

Play at your own pace or enable a 30-second countdown for each question. The quiz moves on automatically when time runs out. The timer is off by default and remembers your choice. Sound feedback is on by default and can be switched off. With sound enabled, gentle beeps mark the final five seconds.

Bible Quiz Certificate Generator
Create A4 PDF participation certificates for your Bible quiz competition. Add multiple participant names at once to generate a separate certificate for each person. Choose English or another supported Bible language, select the date, and add your competition title and optional organization name. Share one certificate or all PDFs together.

Verse images to save and share
Create meaningful Scripture images with nature backgrounds, including flowers, birds, coastlines and starry skies. Backgrounds rotate as you browse. Save your favorites or share a verse image.

Daily Bible verse cards

Draw an encouraging Bible verse whenever you need a quiet moment. Each card gives Scripture a clear, beautiful place to read and reflect.

Daily Hope and reminders

Begin the day with a fresh Daily Hope verse. Set a gentle daily Bible reminder for the time that suits your routine.

Favorites and Bible journal notes

Save meaningful verses to your favorites. Add a personal thought, reflection or prayer beside a verse, then return to the Scripture and journal entry together.

Bible translations and languages

Choose from Berean Standard Bible (BSB), Bible in Basic English (BBE), King James Version (KJV), World English Bible (WEB) and supported Malayalam, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Tagalog translations.

More to enjoy

Share Bible verses with family and friends
Choose from elegant card themes
Back up and restore favorites, journal entries and settings
Listen to optional peaceful background music in Daily Hope
Read comfortably on phones and tablets

Every feature is available

Daily verses, Bible quiz, certificate generation, verse images, card themes, reminders, favorites and journal features are available to everyone. Occasional ads support Hope Cards. A one-time Google Play purchase can remove ads permanently; there is no subscription.

Whether you have one quiet minute or want to keep a Bible verse close throughout the day, Hope Cards offers a focused, welcoming space for Scripture.
