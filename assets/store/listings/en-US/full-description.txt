Pause with Scripture, one card at a time.

Hope Cards is a calm Bible verse app for daily encouragement. Draw a card when you need a thoughtful verse, begin the day with Daily Hope, and keep meaningful Scripture close without a complicated reading plan.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Draw encouraging Bible verse cards
• Read a fresh Daily Hope verse each day
• Choose from multiple Bible translations and languages
• Save favorite verses for later
• Add journal notes linked to each verse
• Set a daily Bible reminder at the time you choose
• Share verses with family and friends
• Choose from elegant card themes
• Back up and restore favorites, journal entries, and settings
• Listen to optional peaceful background music in Daily Hope

EVERY FEATURE IS FREE

All reading, themes, reminders, favorites, and journal features are available free. Occasional ads help support Hope Cards. If you prefer an uninterrupted experience, a one-time Google Play purchase removes ads forever—there is no subscription.

Whether you have one quiet minute or want to return to a verse throughout the day, Hope Cards gives Scripture a focused, welcoming place on your phone or tablet.
